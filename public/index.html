Welcome to SHERM Emergency Command Center

Hospital emergency departments face critical delays during patient surges. SHERM is a full-stack centralized command center with persistent SQLite storage, real-time Server-Sent Events, heuristic resource allocation, and clinical handover management.

📲 Broadcast Emergency Notification

Emergency Dispatch Channels: Sends simultaneous high-priority broadcast to on-call surgical teams, ICU intensivists, and trauma bays via WhatsApp, SMS, and Email.
